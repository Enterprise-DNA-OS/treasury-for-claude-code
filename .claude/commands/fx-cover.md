---
description: Exposure against hedge cover for every currency and time band, against the board-approved policy bands.
---

1. Run `npm run treasury --silent -- fx-cover`.
2. Lead with any band under or over policy, in words: "USD receipts 6 to 12 months out are 13% hedged; policy says 25% to 75%".
3. Work out what would bring it into band: the amount to hedge to reach the minimum, and which forward dates. That is a suggestion for the treasurer, not a trade.
