# Research notes (checked 2026-10-17)

- Kyriba publishes no price list. Each deployment is quoted through sales by modules, bank accounts, entities and payment volume.
- Third-party estimates vary widely: Vendr reports a median of about US$26,600 a year from a small sample (vendr.com/marketplace/kyriba); VendorBenchmark puts mid-market first-year contracts at US$100,000 to US$180,000 (vendorbenchmark.com/vendors/kyriba-treasury-pricing). Neither is a vendor price.
- Smaller treasury tools publish prices, for example TreasuryView at EUR 250 and EUR 500 a month (treasuryview.com/en/pricing-and-plans).
- Hedge accounting documentation: NZ IFRS 9 and AASB 9 paragraph 6.4.1(b) and (c)(iii) (standards.aasb.gov.au/node/2336).
- The work this repo covers is the weekly treasury routine of a mid-sized NZ or AU exporter: daily cash position, a 13-week forecast, FX cover against board-approved bands, facilities and covenants, counterparty limits and bank mandates.
